# Open-source scope

Lucreția Runtime is the reusable infrastructure layer.

Included in the open-source project:
- runtime orchestration;
- local and hybrid worker contracts;
- ComfyUI integration and workflow patching;
- state backup/recovery;
- provider adapter interfaces and safe examples;
- validation, composition, diagnostics and tests;
- generic creative examples used to exercise the runtime.

Not included:
- commercial product logic, personas, customer data or private business workflows;
- private prompts or assets belonging to users;
- credentials or paid-provider account configuration;
- generated media and model weights;
- proprietary business data.

The OSS runtime may be used by commercial products, but those products remain separate from this repository and are not licensed merely because they integrate with Lucreția Runtime.
